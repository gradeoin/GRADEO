SEO Strategy

Priority 1
🔍
Programmatic SEO pages
Create one page per university (VTU, KTU, AKTU, Mumbai, Anna, Pune, JNTU…) with specific grading rules, examples, and a pre-configured calculator embed.
Planned — Q2 2025

Long-form content cluster
10 pillar articles (2,000–4,000 words each) targeting high-volume queries: "how to calculate SGPA", "CGPA to percentage", "minimum marks calculator".

Internal link architecture

Product Improvements

Backlog + Planned
⚠️
Critical bugs / UX issues
Navigation bug fixed (v11 — _navigating flag). Session avg is 2.1min — need to push above 3.5min. Mobile tile tap area too small on 320px screens. PDF export occasionally misaligned on iOS Safari.
🤖
AI CGPA forecasting
Enter your performance pattern → AI predicts likely CGPA range based on current trajectory. Uses Claude API or local lightweight model.
Q3 2025
📊
Analytics dashboard
Per-subject performance charts. Semester-over-semester CGPA trend. Radar chart comparing your profile vs target. Shareable graphic.
Q2 2025
🎓
University auto-detect
Detect university from email domain or manual selection — auto-configure grading scale, credit rules, NSS handling. Removes user confusion.
Q2 2025
📤
Shareable grade card
Public link to your grade card (opt-in). Useful for job applications, internship portals. Badge embed for LinkedIn bio.
Q3 2025
💬
Study group mode
Anonymous CGPA comparison with batchmates. "Your CGPA is in top 20% of students who used GRADEO from your uni." Drives virality.
Idea — 2026
🔔
Exam reminder notifications
Add your exam schedule → push notification 2 days before saying "You need 68 marks in tomorrow's DSA final to hit 8.5 CGPA."
Q4 2025
🌏
Multi-language support
Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Telugu UI translations for better reach in regional engineering colleges. Start with Tamil (largest market).
Idea — 2026
📸
Marksheet photo scan
Upload photo of printed mark sheet → OCR extracts marks automatically → prefills calculator. Huge UX win, reduces friction from 60s to 10s.
Idea — 2026
🔐
Session expiry fix
Firebase auth tokens expire silently. User gets logged out without knowing. Add proactive refresh and graceful re-auth prompt.

2027 Vision

Long-term
🏫
GRADEO for Institutions
White-label GRADEO for engineering colleges — faculty dashboards, cohort analytics, early warning system for at-risk students. First B2B revenue stream.
Vision
🌍
Pan-Asia expansion
Expand to Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Nepal, Malaysia — all have similar university grading systems. Adapt for local credit rules.
Vision
🤝
Career integration
Connect to internship/job portals. "Your CGPA qualifies for 43 companies on Internshala." Creates flywheel from tool → career platform.
Vision
📡
Live result integration
University result scrapers — when VTU publishes results, GRADEO auto-updates your CGPA. Requires partnerships or public data APIs.
Vision
📌
North Star metric
1M+ engineering students use GRADEO to plan at least one exam. That's ~10% of India's active engineering student population. Every feature decision should serve this number.
