FAQ

What is GRADEO?
A free academic planner for engineering students. Replaces manual SGPA calculations and helps you plan exactly what you need to score to hit your target CGPA.
How does the Planner work?
Enter your current CGPA, target CGPA, and internal marks. GRADEO calculates the exact minimum final exam score needed per subject.
Does it support NSS subjects?
Yes. Activity subjects carry 1 fixed credit and a single final grade. GRADEO handles this correctly so your SGPA is accurate.
Is GRADEO free?
Completely free. No sign-in needed to calculate. Sign in with Google to unlock cloud autosave and PDF export. No ads, ever.
What is the student offer?
Sign up with Google and GRADEO unlocks cloud save + PDF export free. No payment required — permanent free offer for students.
