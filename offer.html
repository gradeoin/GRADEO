Questions

Is it really free? What's the catch?
No catch. GRADEO is built by a student for students. The "offer" framing exists because we want to make it feel like the gift it is. There's no premium tier, no hidden charges, no trial that expires. Sign in once and the perks are yours forever.
What data do you store when I sign in?
Only your Google user ID, name, and email — stored in Firebase. Your calculator data (marks, credits, CGPA) is also stored so it syncs across your devices. We never sell any of this. We never show you ads based on it.
Can I use GRADEO without signing in?
Yes, completely. All three calculators work without any account. Sign-in only unlocks cloud save and PDF export — both of which require us to store something on our end.
What if I want to delete my account?
Email us at info@gradeo.in and we'll delete your account and all associated data within 48 hours. No questions asked.
Does this work for all Indian universities?
GRADEO works for any 10-point grading university. VTU, KTU, AKTU, Mumbai, Anna, Pune, JNTU and more have been tested. If your university uses a different scale, let us know via feedback — we'll add support.
