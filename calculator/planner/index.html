HOW THE PLANNER WORKS

The GRADEO engine uses Weighted Academic Gap Analysis (WAGA). It doesn't just divide your target equally; it considers the credit weight of each subject and your current cumulative standing. By marking subjects as "Hard," our logic re-distributes the load to "Easy" subjects, ensuring you hit your goal with the path of least resistance.

This tool is specifically optimized for university systems like VTU, KTU, Anna University, and AICTE standards.
