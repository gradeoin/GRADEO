Gradeo is built for students, not advertisers. Here's exactly what we do and don't do with your data.

Your connection is encrypted
Gradeo runs on HTTPS with TLS encryption — the same standard used by your bank. Everything between your phone and our server is fully encrypted and cannot be read by anyone else.
What we collect
Your name and email (only if you sign in)
Used only to save your grade plans to the cloud and sync across devices. Never shared with anyone.
Grade data you enter (only if you sign in)
Stored in Firebase (Google's secure cloud) to enable cloud sync. Encrypted in transit and at rest.
Anonymous usage analytics
Page views and basic usage data via Cloudflare — no personal identifiers, no tracking across sites.
What we never do
Sell your data
We have never and will never sell your personal information to advertisers or third parties.
Show ads
Gradeo has no ads. No banners, no pop-ups, no sponsored content. It's free because we want it to be.
Track you across other sites
No cross-site tracking, no cookies placed on external sites, no advertising network participation.
Require sign-in to use
You can calculate SGPA, CGPA and plan grades completely anonymously. Sign-in only needed for cloud save.
Store your grade data without sign-in
If you don't sign in, nothing leaves your device. All calculations happen locally in your browser.
Infrastructure & security
Hosted on Cloudflare Pages
Global CDN with DDoS protection, WAF, and automatic HTTPS. Your data loads from the nearest server worldwide.
Authentication via Firebase (Google)
Sign-in is handled by Google's Firebase Auth — we never see or store your Google password.
Data stored in Firestore
Google Cloud Firestore with encryption at rest. Data stored in secure, SOC 2 compliant data centers.
Delete your data anytime
Email us at info@gradeo.in to delete your account and all associated data permanently.

Questions about your privacy or data? We're a small team and we actually read every email.
